A report is a lead. Sometimes the most useful thing in the record is the reason to leave a page off the shelf.

    Phishing field guide

    Read the whole address

    On a phone, tap the address bar to expand it. Compare the hostname with the one in your bookmark. In the Allegro capture, the familiar name is the first thing you see. The address ends in aukcja758487593[.]top.

    Check the request elsewhere

    A logo is easy to copy. If a message asks you to sign in, pay, or fix an account problem, open the app yourself or call a number you already trust. You can check the claim without using the sender’s link.

    If you entered details

    Open the service directly, change the exposed password and any reused copies, and review active sessions. For payment details, call the number printed on your card.

    Report a suspicious message

    In the US, forward scam texts to 7726. You can also report fraud to the FTC or read its phishing guide.
