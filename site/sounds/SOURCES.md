# Broadcast sound effects

Both recordings are offered under [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/).

- `crt-switch.mp3`: LukaCafuka, [Switching a CRT on (2)](https://freesound.org/people/LukaCafuka/sounds/751517/). Excerpt 0.20–0.66 s of the publicly available HQ MP3 preview.
- `vhs-rewind.mp3`: telescapes, [VHS_Rewind](https://freesound.org/people/telescapes/sounds/837702/). Excerpt 0.45–1.35 s of the publicly available HQ MP3 preview.

Both excerpts were downmixed to mono, resampled to 44.1 kHz, peak-scaled, faded at the edges and encoded as 96 kb/s MP3. No Mixkit assets are included.
